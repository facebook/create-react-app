Toyota Rumion on self drive rental

The Rumion is a seven-seater that runs on petrol, and that combination is why people take it. For a family group staying inside the district it costs noticeably less to run over a day than a diesel seven-seater, without giving up the seats. Ask for a seven seater self drive car in Nagercoil or Kanyakumari and this is the petrol one.

Who the Rumion suits

Family groups of five to seven, temple circuits, and weekend trips where everybody wants to travel in one vehicle rather than two.

What it is like to drive here

It drives much like a large hatchback rather than like a van — the controls are light and it is not intimidating to place on a narrow road. The middle row is the comfortable one; the third row is genuinely usable for children and for adults over shorter distances. On the coast road to Colachel and the run out to Kanyakumari it is an easy, unhurried vehicle.

  • Seven seats without diesel running costs
  • Light to drive for its size
  • The middle row slides, so you can trade legroom between rows two and three

Worth knowing

With all seven seats up the boot is small — enough for a day out, not for seven people's luggage. For a wedding party or a multi-day tour with bags, the Innova is the one.

Questions about the Rumion

Can seven adults actually sit in a Rumion?
Seven adults fit for a trip across the district. For a long day, the honest arrangement is five adults and two children, or six adults with the middle row slid back. The third row is a real seat, not a token one, but it is the smallest.
Rumion or Innova for a family trip?
Rumion if you are staying in the district and watching the fuel bill — it is petrol and lighter. Innova if you are going further, carrying luggage for everybody, or want the most comfortable third row. We will tell you which is free on your dates either way.
Is there luggage space with all seven seats in use?
Enough for a day out — bags, water, a couple of small cases. Not enough for seven people's suitcases. Fold the third row and the boot becomes large, which is how most people use it: six travelling, or seven with light bags.
Can I rent a seven seater self drive car in Kanyakumari?
Yes — the Rumion or the Innova, whichever is free on your dates. Both are delivered to Kanyakumari: to the railway station, to a hotel near the beach, or wherever you are staying. The Rumion is the petrol one and costs less over a day inside the district; the Innova is the one for luggage and long days.
